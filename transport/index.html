Transport

Our server is built with transportation in mind! Whether you're a rail fan who loves a detailed network or someone who just enjoys a relaxing tram ride, we've got you covered.

Travel In Style

Our server features realistically designed railways and infrastructure, complete with voice announcements and proper signage to make every journey feel immersive. We even have a realistic fare based system, called Tap2Go, which adds another sense of realism!

Built for Convenience

From detailed tracks to fully functional seating, every transport system is designed to feel authentic and seamless. So whether you're here to explore the network, enjoy the views, or just find the fastest route, Sylvarion City Server has something for every kind of traveler! We even have an International Association of Railways, which is a governed group for consistency between railways on our partnered servers!

Multiple Options

Hop on a fast-moving train or take it easy on one of our trams. Either way, you'll experience a smooth, enjoyable ride that makes traveling fun.
Freight trains will also be possible to drive for a job in the near future so keep an eye out!

Our Transport Options

High Speed Railway

Our high speed rail networks prioritise your journey being quick and efficient to and from point A and B. This service is a great way to go far distances between cities etc. You could take a train ride from Sylvarion Central Station to Portsmouth central! Most of these lines are also scenic too which adds to the fun (even if you are going up to 325km/h!)

Metro

Trams

We have an expansive tram network (Currently in the works) which allows a relaxing and pleasant journey between suburbs and neighborhoods. Major high traffic lines are run on an automatic schedule, but the less traffic lines are able to be driven by tram driving qualified players! (you can apply here), which they earn money by driving, even when no one is riding it!). (WIP Feature).  

International Railways

We have international railways which operate between our partnered servers in the UEIA, such as to Limaru! These work by actually transporting between servers at the edge of the line on each server, (complicated to explain, but trust us, it does work!). These lines offer a sense of uniqueness as not many servers have these features, and it definitely makes the city server experience more whole!

Road Transport

Our road transport includes cars, buses, bikes, and motorbikes, giving everyone plenty of ways to get around. The roads are well looked after and nicely decorated, making travel more enjoyable. Whether you're using motorways, dual carriageways, or smaller roads, getting from place to place is smooth and easy.

Air Transport / Flights

Airplanes in unmodded minecraft? What!? - Yep, thats right! We have working airlines in our server with seats you can go in. This includes the whole airport experience, and while more expensive to buy tickets for, it assures that you can go between large distances faster than any rail network can! On top of that, you can also enjoy a luxury view from your plane window, and see the world below!

& much more!
