Start Guide

Sylvarion has a lot to offer, making it a bit of a overwhelming task to figure out where to start and what to do. This is why we have a nice guide below to help you start your adventure on Sylvarion City Server!

New Player Start Guide

Sylvarion City Server is a Minecraft Java edition server, which means you need to own a copy of this version on either Windows, Linux, or Mac.

  • You can join Sylvarion with the IP: sylvarion.ueia.net
  • Our server version is 26.3, So make sure your client is 1.21.11 or above.
  • We do not support cross-play with Bedrock Edition and have no plans to do so, due to the complexity of our server system.

Official Sylvarion Optimised Modpack

You can download the official modpack compiled by us if you would like the recommended full and performant experience. We recommend that you install this via Modrinth or PrismLauncher. Click the button below to download the latest official version and import it into your launcher!

Server Resource Pack

In order to see our features such as working rideable trains and other vehicles, as well as our exclusive custom block experience, make sure you have Resource Packs Enabled and accept it on the first join!
- We recommend using a Fabric based client with the sodium mod installed for the best performance experience.

Permissions & Rank

For you to be able to unlock the full capabilities of a Sylvian player, you must complete the Citizen Application at spawn. Upon completion, you will automatically be promoted to the Citizen rank which allows you to for example start your own business and buy a shop, as well as other features.

Join our discord

If you haven't already, joining our discord server is a great way to stay up-to-date with the latest features and events, as well as stay connected with the rest of the community. You won't be able to chat in the discord though until you complete the Citizen Application, so make sure you complete that, and follow the instructions within the application to get full access to the Discord.

Some History

Sylvarion's journey is truly unique. Born from a merger of three origin servers—Sunrise, Feria, and Noctora—it was created to foster community progress and engagement. The initial version (v1) focused solely on the central city, but expansion soon proved essential. This led to the development of v2, a more ambitious world that took over a year of effort to create. As of October 26th 2025, Sylvarion has officially launched in Early Access/Beta, inviting players to explore this remarkable vision.

Realistic Transportation

Sylvarion features trams, trains, buses, cars, and bikes, all with interconnected routes, to provide a dynamic travel experience. With a community which has a deep passion for rail networks and innovative infrastructure, this environment is designed to bring realism and creativity together, offering you an immersive journey through the city’s intricate systems. City Jobs & Roleplay

In Sylvarion, there are countless exciting opportunities to earn money and immerse yourself in realistic city roleplay. Become an entrepreneur by buying or renting shop buildings to sell your own products, sourced from factories and farms that you can purchase and manage. Alternatively, explore other unique ways to generate income—many of which are currently in development to expand your options even further!

Server Events

Sylvarion hosts a variety of annual events, including vibrant Summer festivals and spooky Halloween fairs. These events feature exciting main attractions and a wide range of activities to enjoy. Many include fully functional, rideable fairground rides, as well as rentable market stalls where you can sell your goods and earn money, adding a dynamic and immersive touch to the festivities.
